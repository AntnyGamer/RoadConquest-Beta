# RoadConquest 1.0 Beta 4

- Restore the classic in-map car marker to the original pre-Beta-3 silhouette while keeping the
  progression/cosmetic system intact.
- Reduce maximum map zoom from 22 to 20 so the map cannot zoom in quite as far.
- Redesign Settings into elevated rounded cards for Map & appearance, Tracking,
  Account & competition, Progress & data, and Privacy & about.
- Give Settings actions a clearer rounded row treatment and cleaner spacing without changing any
  existing settings behavior.
- Replace the points star symbol with a sword symbol throughout the map, Settings, Achievements,
  and Garage & Shop.
- Keep the Beta 3 points economy, place discovery, achievements, gold UI, road colors,
  export/migration, and battery/performance optimizations unchanged.

Android version code is 30. Android 12 or newer is required.
